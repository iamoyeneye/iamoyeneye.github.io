Power BI Project

Emergency Department
Operational Performance Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to provide operational visibility into patient volumes, waiting times and patient satisfaction.

Project Overview

This project involved developing an interactive operational performance dashboard for an emergency department. The dashboard provides a consolidated view of patient volumes, average waiting times and recorded patient satisfaction, with the ability to analyse performance by reporting month, specialty, day and time period.

The objective was to make areas of operational pressure easier to identify and to provide a clear reporting view that could support further investigation and decision-making.

Interactive Dashboard

Use the controls within the report to explore performance by reporting month, specialty, day and time period.

Key Insights

  • Average waiting time exceeded the 30-minute practice target across several day and time combinations.
  • The highest observed average waiting time in the March 2020 all-specialty view was 49 minutes on Wednesday between 12pm and 3pm.
  • Patient volume increased by 17.4% compared with the previous month in the March 2020 view.
  • Average waiting time decreased by 2.2% compared with the previous month despite the increase in patient volume.

Data Model

The report uses a structured data model centred on the emergency department activity dataset, supported by a dedicated calendar dimension and a time-band sorting dimension.

Dedicated DAX measures were created for patient volume, average waiting time, minimum and maximum waiting time, patient satisfaction and month-over-month comparisons.

Data Quality & Assumptions

Patient satisfaction is calculated from records containing a recorded satisfaction score. Blank satisfaction values are excluded from the average.

The 30-minute waiting-time threshold shown in the wait-time analysis page of the report is a practice assumption used for analytical demonstration. It should not be interpreted as an official performance standard.

This portfolio version of the dataset has been sanitised by removing direct name fields before publication.
